It is an open question how each of our doctoral programs should respond to AI developments. Should we change our courses? Expectations for dissertations? The way we advise? We might also be considering different timelines for change as we wait to see how the story of math and AI unfolds. This site gathers resources for graduate directors. Below are resources that exist and resources that we’re looking for.

Please get in touch to say what would be helpful to add to the wishlist and with your own contributions!
